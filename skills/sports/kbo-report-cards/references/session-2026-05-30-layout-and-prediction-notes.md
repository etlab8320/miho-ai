# 2026-05-30 Layout and Prediction Notes

These notes capture the user-facing KBO card preferences that were clarified during the 2026-05-30 rebuild.

## Review card layout

- Use only the day’s main protagonist as the visual anchor when the story is centered on one player.
- Do not force a fixed photo size.
- Decide vertical vs horizontal photo placement from the amount of copy and the story shape.
- Prefer 기사컷 / 경기장면 / 액션샷 over static headshot-style photos.
- Do not add editorial or meta phrases into the card copy.
- Use baseball facts and concise recap language only.

## Prediction card layout

- The prediction card should not feel like a blind guess.
- Show the key players or starter matchup with photos when available.
- Include an evidence block that names the record signals behind the pick.
- The evidence block should mention:
  - likely starter comparison
  - recent 5-game flow
  - lineup candidates from the record table
  - bullpen workload
  - park/weather if relevant
- Let the key player photo block act as the visual anchor.
- The written rationale should be compact, but the evidence must be visible.

## QA requirement

- Always verify that the rendered HTML and PNG both exist.
- Check that important images load before screenshot.
- Rebuild if the visual is broken, clipped, or missing the intended photo anchor.
