# Miho product promo copy and layout notes

Source: session review of Miho AI promo landscape map.

## Copy preferences to preserve
- Make the product name **미호AI** the primary headline or label, not a secondary tag.
- If the concept references a gumiho/fox identity, express it as **구미호의 감각, 맥락을 읽는 힘, 예리한 판단, 끝까지 처리하는 실행력**.
- Avoid gimmicky or over-cute slogans like “당신을 홀리는 비서” or awkward verb phrases like “말을 알아듣고 끝내는”.
- Keep the tone like a **product introduction / brand promo**, not a message to the user or a developer note.
- Remove user-specific org terms unless the user explicitly wants them in the poster.

## Layout and verification notes
- The top profile panel and the lower card section must be visually separated; if they touch or overlap, increase the vertical spacing or total canvas height and re-render.
- Verify the final PNG by vision, not just by HTML inspection.
- Ensure the main product name is visible in at least two places: headline and one supporting label/card.
- Use color emphasis on the product name and 1–2 key phrases, but keep the page clean and readable.
