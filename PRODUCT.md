# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Miho is used by operators and builders who run an installable AI agent from the
CLI, TUI, dashboard, and messaging platforms such as Discord. Users are often
checking sessions, changing model/provider settings, managing plugins, reading
logs, or monitoring gateway state while Miho works in the background.

## Product Purpose

Miho wraps the portable Miho Agent engine with a branded, approachable operating
surface. The dashboard should make agent operations feel clear, trusted, and
alive: a practical control room for configuration, sessions, plugins, logs,
models, cron jobs, and platform status.

## Brand Personality

Bright, capable, companionable. Miho should feel like a skilled AI partner with
its own identity, not a generic dark developer console. The product can carry a
subtle character presence, but task clarity stays more important than decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid anonymous Nous/Hermes visual leftovers when a screen is meant to be Miho
branded. Avoid one-note dark terminal dashboards, heavy neon cyberpunk, generic
SaaS gradients, decorative glass panels, and mascot art that competes with dense
operational content.

## Design Principles

1. Keep Miho recognizable: brand moments should use Miho-specific imagery and
   teal/jade accents rather than borrowed engine artwork.
2. Preserve operator speed: navigation, status, errors, and settings must stay
   readable before they become expressive.
3. Prefer bright trust over dark mystery: offer light, calm variants for long
   sessions and daylight use.
4. Let personality live in the shell: background art and small accents can carry
   warmth while components remain familiar.
5. Maintain local-first confidence: security, logs, and config surfaces should
   feel controlled and inspectable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Keep motion decorative and
non-essential, respect reduced-motion settings, and make theme changes usable on
both desktop and mobile without hiding information behind low-contrast artwork.
